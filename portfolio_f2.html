Victorian Crash Severity Classifier

Predicts Fatal / Serious / Minor road crash outcomes from Victoria's crash data using a hierarchical two-stage XGBoost classifier built with tidymodels — chosen over a flat model to balance macro-F1 against Fatal-class recall.

RtidymodelsXGBoost

CIFAR-10 Image Classification Comparison

Head-to-head comparison of PCA+Linear SVM, a tuned MLP ensemble, and a CNN on CIFAR-10 — with full hyperparameter tuning, colour-space experiments, and confusion-matrix/ROC-AUC evaluation. The tuned CNN reached 74.6% test accuracy, roughly double the best classical baseline.

PythonTensorFlowscikit-learn

GridPulse

Real-time visualisation of Australia's electricity network — a two-stage pipeline that ingests live power, emissions, price, and demand data from the NEM and streams it over MQTT to an interactive live map, sized by output and colour-coded by fuel type.
